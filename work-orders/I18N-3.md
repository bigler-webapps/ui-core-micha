# I18N-3 — Backfill Swahili in `messagingTranslations` and restore the four-locale shape test

**Target repo:** `ui-core-micha` (branch `main`)
**Tier:** 3 — shared-core, and it re-tightens a test the estate deliberately loosened.

---

## A. Envelope

### Goal

`src/i18n/messagingTranslations.ts` carries **116 keys with `de`/`fr`/`en` and zero `sw`**. Every one of the
other six bundles carries all four locales:

| bundle | de | fr | en | sw |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `auth` | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| `charts` | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 |
| **`messaging`** | **116** | **116** | **116** | **0** |
| `notifications` | 30 | 30 | 30 | 30 |
| `onboarding` | 27 | 27 | 27 | 27 |
| `sectionNav` | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| `userMenu` | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

`I18N-1` hit this while writing its aggregate test: the required "every value keeps its `{de,fr,en,sw}` shape"
assertion failed, the gap was confirmed as pre-existing rather than caused by the merge, and the operator
scoped the test down to `{de,fr,en}` with the backfill recorded as a follow-up. **The follow-up was never
written, and a scoped-down test tends to stay scoped down.** That is what this WO closes.

### Scope

1. **`src/i18n/messagingTranslations.ts`** — add `sw` to all 116 keys.
2. **`tests/i18nAggregate.test.js`** — restore the shape assertion to `{de,fr,en,sw}` and **remove the comment
   recording the scope-down**, since it no longer describes the state.
3. Nothing else. No other bundle is touched; they already conform.

### The translation quality question, which is this WO's real risk

**Machine-translated UI strings that nobody on the team can check are a worse outcome than an honest gap.**
Messaging strings are user-facing and include the kinds of phrase that go wrong invisibly — actions,
confirmations, error states, relative time. So:

- **Name the source of the translations in the register note.** If they are machine-produced, say so plainly
  rather than shipping them as if reviewed.
- **Flag any key the translator was unsure of** rather than guessing. A `sw` value that is wrong is harder to
  find later than one that is missing, because the shape test will then pass.
- **Do not invent terminology.** Where `notifications` or `onboarding` already has a Swahili term for the same
  concept — send, reply, unread, conversation, attachment — **reuse their wording** rather than coining a
  second one. Those bundles are the existing precedent and consistency across them matters more than any
  individual phrasing.

### Who this is actually for

`sw` is configured as a locale in **hram** and in no other app measured. hram does **not** register
`messagingTranslations` today, so nothing is currently broken for a user — this is prevention, and the honest
framing is: it closes an inconsistency and re-arms a test, rather than fixing a live defect.

That matters for the effort trade-off, and it is why the quality bar above is not negotiable: there is no
urgency justifying unreviewed strings.

### Non-goals / do not touch

- **The other six bundles.** They conform.
- **The aggregate itself, the directory ratchet, or the key/value fidelity and collision checks.** Only the
  locale-shape assertion changes.
- **`de`/`fr`/`en` values.** Not a re-translation pass.
- **Adding an eighth locale**, or any change to how consumers register strings (`I18N-2`'s territory).

### Risks

- **116 unreviewable strings.** The mitigation is disclosure, not confidence — see above.
- **Re-tightening the test is the irreversible half.** Once the assertion demands four locales, any future
  bundle addition must supply `sw` or fail CI. That is the intent, and it is worth stating so the next person
  adding a bundle is not surprised.
- **A push to `main` publishes.** See Part C.

### Required tests to WRITE

None new — **`tests/i18nAggregate.test.js`'s restored four-locale assertion IS the test**, and re-arming it is
scope item 2. It will fail until the backfill is complete, which is the correct behaviour and the reason it
belongs in the same change.

Scoped run: `i18nAggregate` plus the messaging export specs. Not the full suite — no dependency or theme
change.

### Parity guardrail

No API, behaviour or visual change for `de`/`fr`/`en` consumers. Purely additive.
